# The PuhuScribe Curriculum

**A framework-first Finnish A1 course — designed zero-to-speaking, ordered the way
acquisition actually works.**

by Fumnanya · for PuhuScribe · companion to `FRAMEWORK.md`

---

## 0. Why this document exists (and what changed)

The first version of the PuhuScribe curriculum was reverse-engineered from a set of **SKK1
class notes**. Those notes were only ever meant to show *how far an A1 classroom had
progressed* — not to serve as the blueprint for the whole course. Because class notes are
grammar notes, a curriculum built from them inherited a grammar-syllabus shape: a long
RULES spine with real communication deferred to the end.

`FRAMEWORK.md` gave us the yardstick. Measured against it, the old curriculum failed in two
concrete ways:

1. **Macro:** the 24 sessions marched through verb types and cases in grammar order — RULES
   first.
2. **Micro:** even inside one session, the flow was *Theory → Drills → Speaking →
   Vocabulary* — i.e. **rules before words**, the exact inverse of WORDS → RULES → REPS →
   FLOW.

This document rebuilds the curriculum from scratch on three commitments:

- **Words first** — both across the course and inside every single session.
- **Communication-goal-driven** — every unit is a thing you can *do*, not a grammar topic.
- **Complete and standalone** — a proper Finnish A1 course (zero → A2 entry), sourced from
  open references and frequency data, *not* bound to anyone's class notes.

---

## 1. Sources — and the copyright line we hold

The curriculum's *coverage* (which words, which verbs, which topics) is drawn from:

- **CEFR A1** can-do descriptors — the international standard for "what an A1 speaker can
  do."
- **uusikielemme.fi** — 650+ free Finnish grammar articles (the project's standing
  reference).
- **Frequency data** — high-frequency Finnish vocabulary and the core verb set (e.g. the
  "25 most-used verbs": *olla, tehdä, mennä, saada, nähdä, syödä, juoda, ymmärtää, tietää,
  haluta, pitää, ostaa, tulla, ottaa, antaa, sanoa, avata, kysyä, odottaa, auttaa, käyttää,
  löytää, asua, pestä, puhua*).

**On the "Language Blueprints — 200+ Finnish Sheets" bundle (Google Drive):** it is a
genuinely useful coverage checklist, but it is stamped *"Copyright © 2026 Language
Blueprints. All rights reserved."* This triggers the project's standing rule: **no
copyrighted content in the app.** Our line:

- ✅ We mine it for **facts** — *which* verbs are most common, *which* vocabulary themes a
  learner needs, *which* grammatical patterns exist. Facts and frequency lists are not
  copyrightable.
- ❌ We never copy its **expression** — its sheets, charts, tables, layout, or explanatory
  wording. Every word card and explanation in PuhuScribe is authored originally in the
  Origin → Logic → Rule → Exceptions voice.

What the bundle confirms we must cover (all mapped to units in §4): core verbs, pronouns,
the six conjugation patterns, themed vocabulary (food, animals, weather, days, body,
adjectives), the local cases & postpositions, modal verbs, numbers, telling time, consonant
gradation, and negative conjugation.

## 2. The corrected session shape (micro level)

Every session is one communication goal, walked through the framework **in order**. The old
flow put Vocabulary fourth; the new flow leads with it.

| # | Step | Stage | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Words** | WORDS | Meet today's vocabulary **and ready-made chunks** first, with audio. You can say useful things before any grammar. |
| 2 | **Input** | *(Rail 1)* | A short comprehensible scene — the new words used in context, mostly in Finnish at **i+1**, meaning always supported. *(new step — the input rail made explicit)* |
| 3 | **Pattern** | RULES | The rule *behind words you've just used*: Origin → Logic → Rule → Exceptions. Grammar explains, it doesn't gate. |
| 4 | **Reps** | REPS | Drills + error tracking — tune accuracy. |
| 5 | **Speaking** | *(bridge)* | Hear it (TTS), then say it yourself. |
| 6 | **Conversation** | FLOW | Live AI dialogue toward the goal — the engine. |
| 7 | **Can-do check** | *(gate)* | Not "did you memorise the rule" but **"can you now *do* the goal?"** |

Two rails — **comprehensible input** and a **low affective filter** — run under all seven
steps (see `FRAMEWORK.md`, Part 3). Speaking is invited early and errors are treated as
data, never failure.

> The single most important change: **WORDS moves from step 4 to step 1**, and RULES is
> reframed as "here's the pattern behind what you already say."

## 3. The macro arc — four phases

The full A1 course spirals through the framework. Each phase has a **centre of gravity**,
but every unit still contains all four stages via the session shape above.

| Phase | Units | Centre of gravity | The learner goes from… |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · Survival** | 1–5 | **WORDS** | zero → communicating in chunks (greet, introduce, count, shop, order) |
| **2 · The Engine** | 6–11 | **RULES** (explaining words already used) | chunks → understanding the verb & case systems behind them |
| **3 · Precision** | 12–18 | **REPS** | rough accuracy → reliable accuracy on the hard systems |
| **4 · Flow & Past** | 19–24 | **FLOW** | accurate sentences → real, spontaneous, past-and-present conversation |

Note the shape is the **inverse** of the old curriculum: WORDS and FLOW are no longer
bookends — WORDS opens wide and FLOW is present from Unit 1 and dominates the finish.

## 4. The 24 units (communication missions)

Each unit is a **can-do goal**. "Core words" are frequency-sourced; "Pattern surfaced" is
the grammar that emerges *to explain words the learner is already using*.

### Phase 1 — Survival (WORDS-dominant)

| # | I can… | Core words / chunks | Pattern surfaced |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | greet someone and introduce myself | hei, moi, terve, näkemiin; nimi; *"Minä olen…"*, *"Kuka sinä olet?"*, *"Hauska tutustua"* | *olla* (named as the pattern behind chunks you already say) |
| 2 | say where I'm from and where I live | countries, nationalities, *kotoisin*, *asun…*, *"Mistä olet?"* | locative *-ssa* as a chunk (rule comes later, Unit 8) |
| 3 | talk about my family and what I have | perhe vocab, *minulla on*, numbers 1–10 | possession *minulla on* as a pattern |
| 4 | count, ask prices, and shop | numbers, *euro*, *paljonko*, *ostaa*, market/food words | partitive after numbers (light touch) |
| 5 | order in a café | food & drink, *haluan*, *saisinko*, *kiitos*, café script | partitive object (light touch) |

### Phase 2 — The Engine (RULES-dominant, always explaining used words)

| # | I can… | Core words / chunks | Pattern surfaced |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | describe my daily routine | the 25 core verbs as daily actions | **Verb Type 1** conjugation (you already say *puhun, asun, ostan*) |
| 7 | say what I don't do | same verbs, negative | **Negative conjugation** (en/et/ei + stem) |
| 8 | say where things and places are | places around Helsinki | **Inessive -ssa / Adessive -lla** + vowel harmony |
| 9 | talk about going places | *mennä, käydä, tulla, lähteä* | **Type 2 verbs**; Illative *-Vn* (Mihin?), Elative *-sta* (Mistä?) |
| 10 | tell the time and talk about my week | clock, days, *aamulla/illalla*, frequency | **Time expressions** (a named weak area) |
| 11 | say what I like, want, and can do | hobbies, *pitää + sta*, *haluta*, *voida*, *osata* | **Modal verbs** (modal + infinitive) |

### Phase 3 — Precision (REPS-dominant)

| # | I can… | Core words / chunks | Pattern surfaced |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | use every verb type correctly | mixed verbs | **Verb Types 3–6** consolidated (kuunnella, herätä, valita, tarvita) |
| 13 | make consonants change correctly | high-frequency KPT words | **KPT — full pattern set** (REPS-heavy) |
| 14 | use the partitive when I need it | food, feelings, negation | **Partitive — full picture** (negation, quantity, partial object) |
| 15 | describe people and things | 40 core adjectives, colours | **Adjective agreement** |
| 16 | talk about more than one | everyday nouns | **Plurals** (monikko), partitive plural |
| 17 | show possession and relationships | people, belongings, *kanssa* | **Genitive -n**, *kenen?* |
| 18 | give, receive, and act on objects | *antaa, soittaa, ottaa* | **Allative/Ablative -lle/-lta**; direct object rules |

### Phase 4 — Flow & Past (FLOW-dominant)

| # | I can… | Core words / chunks | Pattern surfaced |
|---|---|---|---|
| 19 | talk about the past | weekend/yesterday vocab | **Past tense (imperfekti)** + past participle (seen earlier in perfect chunks) |
| 20 | understand and use spoken Finnish | *mä, sä, se, ne*; contractions | **Puhekieli** (passive as we-form, vowel drops) |
| 21 | hold a real conversation about my life | integrative — all prior vocab | FLOW session — multi-topic, mostly in Finnish |
| 22 | handle my professional context | industry vocab (photography, design, AI…) | FLOW — Professional Track in real exchanges |
| 23 | do everything so far (review) | mixed | **REPS** consolidation across A1 |
| 24 | speak about myself and my world (A2 entry) | mixed | **FLOW** assessment — can-do based, spoken production |

Every item in the Drive bundle is covered: core verbs → U1/6; pronouns → U1; six
conjugation patterns → U6/9/12; themed vocab → U4/5/10/15; cases & postpositions →
U8/9/17/18; modal verbs → U11; numbers → U4; time → U10; KPT → U13; negative → U7. The
200+ verb list is an ongoing vocabulary well, not a unit.

## 5. One fully-modeled session — Unit 1

This is the template every session follows. Note WORDS leads; the rule arrives *after* the
learner is already using the language.

**Goal (can-do):** *I can greet someone, say my name, ask theirs, and say where I'm from.*

**Step 1 — WORDS (meet these first, with audio)**
- *hei / moi / terve* (hi), *näkemiin* (goodbye), *nimi* (name)
- Chunks, learned whole, usable immediately:
  *"Minä olen ___."* (I am ___) · *"Kuka sinä olet?"* (Who are you?) ·
  *"Mistä sinä olet?"* (Where are you from?) · *"Hauska tutustua."* (Nice to meet you.)

**Step 2 — INPUT (a comprehensible scene, mostly Finnish at i+1)**
> First day of class. *Aino: "Hei! Minä olen Aino. Kuka sinä olet?"* — meaning supported
> inline. The learner hears the chunks doing real work before any rule.

**Step 3 — PATTERN (explain what you've just been saying)**
- *Origin:* every language has one verb holding everything together — in Finnish it's
  *olla*.
- *Logic:* you've already said *minä olen*, *sinä olet*. Those share a system.
- *Rule:* the *ole-* stem + endings.
- *Exception:* ⚠ *hän on* (not *olee*); *he ovat*.

**Step 4 — REPS** — fill-in (*Me ___ kurssilla* → *olemme*), negation (*ei ole*), one
translation.

**Step 5 — SPEAKING** — TTS plays the chunks; learner records the introduction.

**Step 6 — FLOW** — live AI: *Aino* greets the learner; they must introduce themselves and
ask one question back. Two turns minimum.

**Step 7 — CAN-DO CHECK** — *"Introduce yourself to a new classmate and ask where they're
from."* Passing means you can **do the goal**, not recite *olla*.

## 6. Before / after

| | Old (grammar-syllabus) | New (framework-first) |
|---|---|---|
| Unit identity | a grammar topic ("Inessive case") | a can-do goal ("I can say where things are") |
| Course order | verb types & cases in grammar sequence | communication goals; grammar surfaces as needed |
| In-session order | Theory → Drills → Speaking → **Vocabulary** | **Words** → Input → Pattern → Reps → Speaking → Conversation |
| First contact with a structure | the rule | the **words and chunks** that use it |
| FLOW (real conversation) | mostly Units 22–24 | **every unit**, from Unit 1 |
| Source of truth | one set of class notes | CEFR + uusikielemme + frequency data |
| The promise | "study the grammar" | **"follow the path, go from zero to speaking"** — credible because the path is now ordered the way acquisition works |

---

## 7. On the promise

PuhuScribe's promise — *"follow the framework and go from not knowing a word to speaking
Finnish"* — is only honest if the curriculum is actually ordered the way people acquire
language. This redesign is what makes it honest: words first, communication from day one,
rules in service of speech, FLOW throughout.

Keep the claim **credible, not guaranteed**: time-to-speaking varies by person — by how
much comprehensible input they get and how low their affective filter is (Krashen). That is
exactly why the honest version is *"…in a time that's different for everyone,"* not a fixed
number of weeks. The framework lets us promise the **path**, not a stopwatch.
